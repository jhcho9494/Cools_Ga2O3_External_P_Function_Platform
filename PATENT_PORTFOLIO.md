# Patent Portfolio — Ga₂O₃ External P-Function Platform

## 1. Portfolio thesis

The portfolio protects a common technical proposition:

> Gallium oxide retains its high-field n-type semiconductor role, while the unavailable p-type function is supplied by an external functional block.

The family is structured to cover the external p-function at four levels:

1. general material/function substitution,
2. device-specific implementation,
3. high-quality crystalline grafting, and
4. process and thermal-platform integration.

---

## 2. Core patent axes

### Axis A — External p-type oxide region

**Subject:** Gallium-oxide power semiconductor device bypassing the difficulty of p-type Ga₂O₃ doping through an external p-type oxide region.

**Core protected concept:**

- n-type Ga₂O₃ drift region,
- p-type oxide region formed from a material other than Ga₂O₃,
- heterojunction p-n functionality at the interface,
- no native p-doping process in the Ga₂O₃ body.

**Representative functions:** field termination, guard ring, depletion expansion, reverse blocking, and heterojunction rectification.

**Representative p-type oxides:** NiO and Cu₂O.

---

### Axis B — P-body-free normally-off Ga₂O₃ FET

**Subject:** Normally-off Ga₂O₃ transistor using fixed charge or an external p-type oxide without a native p-type body.

**Core protected concept:** zero-bias channel depletion without a native Ga₂O₃ p-body.

**Implementation branches:**

- fixed negative charge in the gate dielectric,
- fixed negative charge at the dielectric/channel interface,
- external p-type oxide under or adjacent to the gate,
- fully depleted thin channel with gate-work-function adjustment,
- combinations of the above.

**Device effect:** positive threshold-voltage shift and fail-safe channel closure at loss of gate drive.

---

### Axis C — External-p-oxide junction-barrier Schottky diode

**Subject:** Ga₂O₃ JBS diode using a p-type oxide grid without native p-type Ga₂O₃ doping.

**Core protected concept:**

- forward current through Schottky windows,
- reverse-bias depletion expansion from p-type oxide grid regions,
- shielding of the Schottky interface,
- simultaneous reduction of forward drop and reverse leakage.

**Design parameters:** grid width, grid depth, Schottky-window spacing, and depletion-width overlap.

---

### Axis D — Grafted single-crystal p-type semiconductor

**Subject:** Ga₂O₃ power device receiving p-functionality by bonding and grafting a separately grown single-crystal p-type semiconductor.

**Core protected concept:**

- separately grown crystalline p-type layer,
- release or layer-transfer from its growth substrate,
- bonding to n-type Ga₂O₃,
- heterojunction p-n function without native p-type Ga₂O₃.

**Candidate grafted materials:** p-Si, p-GaN, and p-diamond.

**Applications:** p-side rectifier region, p-gate, junction termination, guard ring, hole-injection region, and electric-field-control region.

---

### Axis E — Selective photothermal annealing

**Subject:** Selective photothermal processing of Ga₂O₃ power-semiconductor regions on an SiC or SiC-containing support.

**Core protected concept:** optical absorption and heating are concentrated in a designed selective-absorption region rather than in the pure bulk material.

**Selective-absorption targets:**

- heavily doped n-type region,
- ion-implantation damage,
- growth-defect region,
- regrowth interface,
- oxygen-vacancy or defect-level-rich region,
- metal-contact interface,
- optical absorber-assist layer.

**Process outputs:** dopant activation, damage recovery, ohmic-contact improvement, interface electrical-resistance reduction, and interface thermal-resistance reduction.

**Structural fingerprint:** a spatial discontinuity between locally annealed and substantially unannealed regions.

---

### Axis F — RBSC-integrated Ga₂O₃ UWBG device

**Subject:** Single-crystal Ga₂O₃ functional layer integrated with an RBSC heat-dissipating substrate.

**Core protected concept:**

- Ga₂O₃ high-field functional layer,
- single or blocking bonding interface,
- RBSC heat spreading and package support,
- lateral, trench, fin, vertical Schottky, and heterojunction device variants,
- high-voltage application systems.

**Interface branches:** self-formed SiO₂ blocking/bonding interface, ALD blocking interface, auxiliary β-SiC thermal interface, and conductive metal-silicide interface.

## 5. Portfolio positioning

The family does not depend on a single p-type material or a single device geometry. Its strategic coverage is the separation of:

- **Ga₂O₃ high-field n-type function**, and
- **externally supplied p-function**.

The device can therefore select the least complex external p-function needed for each role:

- fixed charge for threshold control,
- p-type oxide for local depletion and field shaping,
- p-type oxide grids for JBS shielding,
- transferred crystalline p-layers for higher-quality hole functionality,
- selective photothermal treatment for local process recovery, and
- RBSC for heat, package, and system integration.
